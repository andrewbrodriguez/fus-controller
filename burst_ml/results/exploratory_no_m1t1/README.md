# Exploratory: without Mouse 1 T1

**Not pre-registered.** Same models and settings as the main run, with Mouse 1 T1 (three sonications at one site, dose 2.44) left out: `bml.run --exclude 1:1 --out results/exploratory_no_m1t1`.

Generated by `bml/report.py` from the CSVs in this folder; do not edit by hand. Plan, method and
reading: [`../README.md`](../README.md). 34 sonicated targets, 6 mice; every score is leave one
animal out (fit on five mice, predict the sixth with a fresh animal effect).
