# Delivery by brain region

Generated by `scripts/region_delivery.py`; do not edit by hand. Mice 1-5; 28 single-sonication targets (Mouse 1's T1, three sonications, and T3, the no-FUS control, are left out).

Regions are the planned targets from the lab's mask: T1/T4 anterior striatum, T2/T5 lateral striatum and cortex, T3/T6 thalamus; T1-T3 on the animal's right.

**Method.** Delivery is put on a logit scale and fitted against log dose with one offset per mouse and a common slope (cells: 1.54, pixels: 1.09). The residual is delivery above or below what that mouse's dose-response predicts. p-values shuffle target labels within each mouse (20,000 permutations), so differences between animals can't drive them.

## Dose by position

## Reading

- **Thalamus is strongest raw, not after dose.** Its targets got the most dose here (mean 1.24 vs 0.86 and 0.90); dose-adjusted, thalamus vs the rest is +0.21 (p 0.62).
- **T1 (right anterior striatum) is lower than T4 (left) at matched dose**, in 4/4 mice on cells (p 0.018) and 4/4 on pixels (p 0.034). It is not a whole-right-side effect: the other two right-left pairs go both ways.

## Caveats

- The lab raised both questions after looking at these same animals' images, so agreement here is not independent. Mice 6+ are the clean test.
- "Region" is the planned target. Sections sit ~1.7-2.7 mm deep (`fus.reconstruct`), above most focal centres (2.9-3.7 mm; thalamic ones deepest), so the tissue in a T3/T6 ROI may not be thalamus.
- From Mouse 2 on, position = firing order: T1 is always fired first and T4 fourth, so the T1-T4 gap could be firing order rather than side. Their doses in the imaged mice are about equal (0.80 vs 0.81 mean).
- 4-5 mice per contrast; three region contrasts and three pairs were tested per measure, uncorrected.
