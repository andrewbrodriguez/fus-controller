# Delivery by brain region

Generated by `scripts/region_delivery.py`; do not edit by hand. Mice 1-6; 34 single-sonication targets (Mouse 1's T1, three sonications, and T3, the no-FUS control, are left out).

Regions are the planned targets from the lab's mask: T1/T4 anterior striatum, T2/T5 lateral striatum and cortex, T3/T6 thalamus; T1-T3 on the animal's right.

**Method.** Delivery is put on a logit scale and fitted against log dose with one offset per mouse and a common slope (cells: 1.47, pixels: 1.10). The residual is delivery above or below what that mouse's dose-response predicts. p-values shuffle target labels within each mouse (20,000 permutations), so differences between animals can't drive them.

## Reading

- **Thalamus is strongest raw, not after dose.** Its targets got the most dose here (mean 1.50 vs 0.89 and 0.82); dose-adjusted, thalamus vs the rest is +0.07 (p 0.85).
- **T1 (right anterior striatum) is lower than T4 (left) at matched dose**, in 5/5 mice on cells (p 0.052) and 5/5 on pixels (p 0.071). It is not a whole-right-side effect: T2-T5 goes both ways, and T3 (right thalamus) reads *higher* than T6 in 5/5 mice on cells (p 0.074).

## Caveats

- The lab raised both questions after looking at these same animals' images, so agreement here is not independent. Mouse 6 was measured after the suggestion (2026-11-16) and is the only animal that tests them cleanly.
- "Region" is the planned target. Sections sit ~1.7-2.7 mm deep (`fus.reconstruct`), above most focal centres (2.9-3.7 mm; thalamic ones deepest), so the tissue in a T3/T6 ROI may not be thalamus.
- From Mouse 2 on, position = firing order: T1 is always fired first and T4 fourth, so the T1-T4 gap could be firing order rather than side. Their doses in the imaged mice are about equal (0.85 vs 0.83 mean).
- 5-6 mice per contrast; three region contrasts and three pairs were tested per measure, uncorrected.
