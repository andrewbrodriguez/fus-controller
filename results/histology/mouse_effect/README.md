# Within-mouse trends and the mouse effect

Generated by `scripts/mouse_effect.py`; do not edit by hand. Delivery is the fraction of neurons GFP+ (ΣGFP+ ÷ ΣNeuN, pipeline B) on a logit scale against log dose, over 35 sonicated targets in 6 mice (Mouse 1's no-FUS control left out).

Figures: `within_mouse_trend.png`, `mouse_level_vs_acoustics.png` (exploratory).

- **Shared slope 1.47** logit per log dose (90%: 0.94–2.00): doubling the dose multiplies the odds of a neuron being GFP+ by 2.8.
- Separate slopes per mouse fit no better than the shared one (F = 1.07, p 0.40). With 5–6 targets per mouse, modest differences could hide.
- **Repeated-measures correlation** (dose vs delivery after removing each mouse's means): cells r +0.67 (df 28, p 5.6e-05); pixels r +0.60 (p 4.3e-04).
- With six targets, a single mouse's r is very uncertain (r = 0.7 has a 90% interval of roughly 0.1–0.95).
