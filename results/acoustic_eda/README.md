# Sonication EDA

Generated by `scripts/sonication_eda.py`. Exploratory: nothing here is a pre-registered test.

## Summary sheet, mice 2–24 (Mouse 1 left out: rotated rows)

- **Design** (`design.png`): 6 programmed burst counts (45, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240) × 6
  harmonic goals (0.55–1.05). Stored counts are programmed + 15 (mice 1–6) or + 12 (mice 7–24).
  The reference cell (120 bursts at goal 0.75) holds 23 targets, and all
  23 mice have at least one.
- **Firing order is position.** For mice 2–24, treatment # equals brain region on every row:
  position 1 is always sonicated first and position 6 last. Order effects (bubble or
  anaesthesia drift) and position effects (anatomy, skull) can't be separated.
- **The design explains most of the dose** (`dose_vs_design.png`): log cumulative 2nd
  harmonic ~ log bursts + goal gives R² = 0.77. The burst exponent is
  0.87; 1.0 would mean dose ∝ bursts.
- **Reference target reproducibility:** cumulative dose CV 22% (range
  0.70–1.73); mean voltage CV 14%.
- **Animal vs target** (`animal_effects.png`, one-way ICC on residuals after the design):
  22% of the residual dose variance is between mice; 41% of the
  residual drive-voltage variance is between mice. A large share of the voltage needed to
  reach a goal is an animal property (skull, coupling), while the dose left over is mostly
  target-level, which supports within-animal contrasts.
- **Voltage and dose residuals** are barely related (r = +0.14): needing
  more voltage than the goal predicts says little about the dose a target ended up with.
- **Mean residual by firing order** (dose, log): {1: -0.019, 2: 0.014, 3: -0.004, 4: 0.016, 5: -0.056, 6: 0.05}. Voltage: {1: -0.027, 2: 0.047, 3: -0.09, 4: 0.041, 5: 0.067, 6: -0.038}.
- **`Mean MPa` is a fixed multiple of voltage:** MPa / V = 4.050 (SD
  0.0e+00). It is a conversion, not a measurement; don't use it as a
  separate predictor.

## Raw recordings, mice 1–6 (37 recordings)

Online harmonic (`HH`) is the controller's own reading, on the goal's scale.
`recordings.csv` has every number per recording.

- **Traces:** `controller_harmonic.png` (online harmonic with the goal band) and
  `controller_voltage.png` (drive voltage with the ceiling).
- **Ramp:** median 8 sonicating bursts to first reach the band
  (range 3–18). Starting voltage
  0.005 V, ceiling 0.125 V.
- **Tracking after the ramp:** median 19% of bursts inside the
  deadband, 21% above, 54% below. The
  band is only ±0.05 wide, while burst-to-burst CV of the online harmonic is median
  20%, so most bursts are expected to fall outside it.
  `controller_summary.png` plots these against goal.
- **The controller settles below its setpoint.** After the ramp, the online harmonic averages
  0.93× the goal midpoint (median over recordings; median-based
  0.92×). This holds at every goal from 0.55 to 1.05, and only
  35% of recordings average inside their band. Voltage is flat by then
  (median slope over the second half ≈ 0), so this is a steady offset, not a slow ramp. It
  isn't skew (median skew 0.28). The realised harmonic is therefore a near-constant
  fraction of the nominal goal, which matters for any model using `goal` rather than the
  measured harmonic.
- **Voltage ceiling:** 2 of 37 recordings reached 0.125 V
  (M4 Target6, M6 Target2). There, the goal
  may not have been achievable.
- **Interlock:** fired in 7 of 37 recordings
  (M1 Target4 (goal 0.90–1.00 → 0.80–0.90, 4 bursts over threshold), M2 Target6 (goal 0.80–0.90 → 0.71–0.81, 5 bursts over threshold), M3 Target3 (goal 0.90–1.00 → 0.85–0.95, 2 bursts over threshold), M3 Target4 (goal 1.00–1.10 → 0.94–1.05, 3 bursts over threshold), M4 Target3 (goal 1.00–1.10 → 0.94–1.05, 5 bursts over threshold), M5 Target5 (goal 1.00–1.10 → 0.89–0.99, 3 bursts over threshold), M6 Target2 (goal 0.60–0.70 → 0.53–0.63, 2 bursts over threshold)).
- **Noise floor** (`noise_floor.png`): within-session CV of the zero-volt 2nd-harmonic level is
  median 7% per mouse. Each recording's dose is normalised by its own floor, so
  floor drift rescales dose directly.
- **Session timing:** median 3.5 min between consecutive sonications.
